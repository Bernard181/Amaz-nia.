
# 🌳 Forest Intelligence

### AI-powered environmental intelligence and knowledge retrieval system.

Forest Intelligence is an experimental Artificial Intelligence project focused on combining Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and environmental knowledge to support research, information analysis, and environmental protection.

The project aims to explore how AI can retrieve, interpret, and organize environmental and legal information from specialized knowledge bases.

> Turning environmental knowledge into actionable intelligence.

---

## 🎯 Project Vision

The main goal of Forest Intelligence is to develop an AI-powered system capable of connecting environmental information, scientific research, and Brazilian environmental legislation.

The system is designed to explore how artificial intelligence can assist researchers, environmental organizations, and other professionals in understanding environmental issues through reliable and traceable information.

## 🧠 Core Technologies

The project explores the following technologies:

- **Python** — Main development language.
- **Qwen** — Large Language Models and embedding models.
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** — Knowledge retrieval and contextual generation.
- **Obsidian** — Knowledge organization and Markdown document management.
- **Vector Databases** — Semantic information retrieval.
- **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** — Text processing and interpretation.
- **Computer Vision** — Future exploration of environmental image analysis.

## 🏗️ Project Architecture

The initial architecture is based on the following components:

1. **Knowledge Base:** Environmental legislation, scientific research, technical documents, and other trusted sources.
2. **Document Processing:** Reading, cleaning, and splitting documents into smaller chunks.
3. **Embedding Engine:** Converting text into numerical vector representations.
4. **Vector Search:** Retrieving relevant information based on semantic similarity.
5. **LLM Integration:** Using Qwen models to interpret retrieved information and generate contextual responses.
6. **Response Generation:** Producing answers with references to the original knowledge sources.

## 📚 Knowledge Base

The initial knowledge base will be organized using Markdown documents and Obsidian.

Potential knowledge domains include:

- Brazilian environmental legislation.
- Forest conservation.
- Deforestation.
- Wildfires and environmental management.
- Illegal mining.
- Biodiversity.
- Scientific publications and technical research.

## ⚖️ Responsible AI

Forest Intelligence is intended to support research and information analysis.

AI-generated outputs must not be treated as definitive legal conclusions, official environmental inspections, or forensic reports.

Information must be evaluated against reliable sources and reviewed by qualified professionals whenever necessary.
